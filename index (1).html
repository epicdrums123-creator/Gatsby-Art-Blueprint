The Gatsby Blueprint

Open Mind Silhouette — Complete Execution Guide — English 3

The core concept

The silhouette is split vertically down the center — two worlds inside one head. The left half represents the dream (who Gatsby performs himself to be). The right half represents the reality (what the dream costs and conceals). The split line is not clean — it bleeds and smears into each other, gold into grey, because Gatsby himself could not keep those worlds separated. That bleed edge is your mom's mixed media playground. Use it.

Inside the silhouette

Left half — the dream

gold · emerald · ivory · warm cream

  • 🟢The green lightThe most iconic Gatsby symbol — his whole dream compressed into one point of light across the water
  • 👔Cascading gold shirtsThe scene where he throws shirts at Daisy — the performance of wealth, the need to be seen
  • 🏛️White mansion facadeWest Egg grandeur — everything built to impress one person
  • 🕰️A stopped clockWhen he almost knocks Nick's clock — his obsession with repeating the past, freezing time at 1917
  • 🍾Champagne glasses, music notesThe parties — noise covering the emptiness, thrown for an audience of one who never came
  • ⭐Stars and light raysThe extraordinary gift for hope — Fitzgerald's final words about the green light
  • ✍️"Old Sport" faint in backgroundRepeated phrase — the performance of old money class he never possessed

Right half — the reality

ash grey · dirty white · muted yellow · black

  • 👁️The eyes of Doctor T.J. EckleburgThe billboard — god watching over a moral wasteland, seeing everything Gatsby pretends is unseen
  • 🌫️Valley of Ashes — grey dust, grey figuresThe cost of the dream — the people ground into ash by old money's indifference
  • 🚗The yellow car (not gold — yellow)Death machine. Daisy was driving. Gatsby took the blame. The dream destroyed everything it touched.
  • 💧The pool — still, dark waterWhere he dies waiting for a call from Daisy that never comes
  • 🪑Empty chairs, empty funeralOnly Nick came — thousands at the parties, nobody at his death. The dream's real verdict.
  • 💀A crumbling version of the mansionSame building as the left side — decayed. What was always underneath the performance.
  • 🌑James Gatz's name — small, fadingWho he actually was before the invention — the person he abandoned and could never escape

The bleed edge — where the halves meet

This is the most important technical decision in the piece. The center line where gold meets ash should not be a hard edge. Have your mom use wet-on-wet technique, or dry brush, or ink bleed — something that lets the colors seep into each other 1–2 inches on each side of center. The green light exists on both sides but fades from vivid to dull as it crosses right. The mansion appears on both sides — beautiful on the left, crumbling on the right. That's the thesis of the book made visual.

Outside the silhouette

  • 🌊Dark navy/black background — East Egg and West Egg water at night. Deep, cold, separating.
  • 🟢One small green light glowing OUTSIDE the silhouette, at the bottom right edge — reaching inward toward his head. The dream exists outside of him, unreachable.
  • 🌉Faint dock shape at the bottom of the silhouette — where Gatsby stood and reached toward the light.
  • 🌙Optional: faint gold line tracing the silhouette's edge — the outline of the performance, not the person.

Grading — how to guarantee all 30 points

10 Story connection

Green light, Eckleburg eyes, yellow car, the pool, the clock — any teacher who read the book will recognize these instantly. The split itself argues the novel's central thesis.

10 Creativity & design

The bleed edge technique, mixed media layers, gold leaf, and the outside green light make this look nothing like a student project.

10 Symbolism & themes

Every symbol maps to a theme — illusion vs reality, old money vs new, the impossibility of repeating the past. Meaning is embedded, not decorative.
